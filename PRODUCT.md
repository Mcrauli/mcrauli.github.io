# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Finnish employers and recruiters in technical design, HVAC engineering, and BIM/IFC coordination; scanning portfolios to assess fit for an internship or junior position. They glance quickly: they want to see what Lauri has built and whether he understands the domain, not read a CV narrative.

## Product Purpose

Lauri Rekola's personal job-seeking portfolio. Communicates CAD/BIM automation work in enough depth to be credible without exposing proprietary project details. Target outcome: a recruiter reaching out or downloading the CV. Secondary outcome: Lauri being identifiable as someone who understands the design engineer's daily workflow, not just a coder who happened to work near one.

## Brand Personality

Technical, precise, quietly confident. Finnish tone: professional, concrete, no promotional language. The work speaks; the words enable it. Three words: grounded, deliberate, capable.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS portfolios: white or near-white background, Inter + blue-accent color scheme, icon + heading + text card grids, section headers like "My Skills" and "My Projects", LinkedIn-adjacent corporate softness.

## Design Principles

1. Show, don't tell. Work is demonstrated through workflows and concrete outcomes, not adjectives.
2. Earn every word. No restated headings, no intros that repeat the title, no filler sentences.
3. Technical identity is the aesthetic. The cyberpunk-neon visual language is not decoration; it signals that Lauri codes and takes craft seriously.
4. Privacy by design. Employer identity and implementation details stay out; the framing of what is missing is as deliberate as what is shown.
5. One audience at a time. Every decision optimizes for the recruiter or hiring manager scanning on a laptop, not general public delight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Reduced motion supported via prefers-reduced-motion. Keyboard navigation required for all interactive elements.
